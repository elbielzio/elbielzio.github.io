My collection, uploaded to CollectionBuilder, is based on archaeological research conducted in various Indigenous communities in the Department of Pando, Bolivia. It features archaeological materials, equipment, and locations that I documented during the summers of 2025 and 2026.

The first element in this collection is an Inka Bottle Ceramic from the 15th century, discovered in a circular archaeological trench, or geoglyph, in the community of Las Piedras. The second element is a photograph of a Drone Matrice 350 RTK, which I used for mapping and analyzing sites in Amazonia and for generating subsequent elements of this collection. The third element is a Digital Elevation Model, which provides information on landscape elevations, depicting a group of geoglyphs in the community of Loma Alta. The fourth element consists of LIDAR data collected from a flight over the same structures in Loma Alta. The final element is a satellite image of geoglyphs in the community of Puerto Rico, where I am currently focusing my dissertation research.

This collection will continue to receive additional images as my PhD research progresses.
