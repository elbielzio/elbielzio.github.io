## Gabriel Santos de Oliveira (He/Him)
{: .profile-name}

Brazilian Anthropologist ● First Generation Scholar  
PhD Student at the University of Virginia
{: .profile-subtitle}

![Gabriel Santos de Oliveira, wearing glasses and a black sweater, smiling with arms crossed and a thumbs-up, in front of green shrubs with a lake and forested hills behind him]({{ '/assets/img/profilepicture.jpg' | relative_url }}){: .profile-photo}

I am a PhD student in Anthropology and hold a BA in History from the University of São Paulo. My research focuses on Amazonian archaeology, examining long-term relationships between Indigenous peoples and sacred forest landscapes in the Department of Pando, Bolivia.

I draw from Historical Ecology and Relational Archaeology approaches to investigate geometric earthwork sites to better understand the deep history and cultural landscapes of Southwestern Amazonia, with particular attention to processes of place-making, the construction of belonging, and memory.

As a first-generation scholar of Indigenous and Afro-descendant heritage from Brazil, I am committed to academic practices that center Indigenous perspectives and emphasize historical continuity.
